GLOBAL CUISINE GUIDE — INGREDIENTS, TECHNIQUES & CULTURAL CONTEXT
==================================================================

SECTION 1: INDIAN CUISINE

1.1 The Spice Pantry
Indian cooking relies on a foundational spice pantry. Key spices and their roles:
- Turmeric (haldi): anti-inflammatory, earthy flavour, golden colour. Add early in cooking.
- Cumin (jeera): warm, nutty. Use whole seeds tempered in oil, or ground in masalas.
- Coriander (dhania): citrusy, slightly sweet. Pairs with cumin in most masalas.
- Garam masala: complex spice blend (typically cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, black pepper, cumin) — add at end of cooking to preserve aroma.
- Chili powder: heat and colour. Kashmiri chili = mild heat, deep red colour. Regular = more heat.
- Mustard seeds: pungent, nutty when tempered in hot oil.
- Fenugreek (methi): bitter, maple-like undertone. Use sparingly.
- Cardamom (elaichi): floral, sweet. Essential in biryanis, desserts, chai.

1.2 Chicken Tikka Masala (adapted for home cook)
Ingredients (Marinade): 500g chicken thighs (cubed), 150g yogurt, 1 tsp each cumin/coriander/turmeric/chili powder, 1 tbsp garam masala, 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, salt.
Ingredients (Sauce): 2 onions (puréed), 400g canned tomatoes (blended), 150ml heavy cream, 2 tbsp butter, 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 1 tsp each coriander/chili powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tbsp garam masala, salt, fresh coriander.
Instructions:
1. Marinate chicken minimum 4 hours (overnight preferred).
2. Grill or bake at 220°C until charred at edges (15-20 min). SAFETY: ensure internal temp reaches 74°C/165°F.
3. For sauce: melt butter, temper cumin seeds, cook onion purée until oil separates (15 min).
4. Add ginger-garlic paste, cook 3 minutes.
5. Add tomato purée, cook 10 minutes until thick.
6. Add spices, cook 2 minutes.
7. Add chicken, simmer 5 minutes.
8. Stir in cream; simmer 5 minutes. Garnish with coriander.
Contains: dairy (cream, butter, yogurt). Gluten-free if served with rice.

1.3 Vegetable Biryani
Fragrant basmati rice layered with spiced vegetables.
Ingredients: 300g basmati rice (soaked 30 min), 400g mixed vegetables (cauliflower, potato, carrot, peas), 2 onions (thinly sliced), 150g yogurt, 3 tbsp ghee, whole spices (1 cinnamon stick, 4 cardamoms, 4 cloves, 2 bay leaves), 1 tsp biryani masala, saffron in warm milk, fresh mint and coriander.
Instructions:
1. Fry onions in ghee until deep golden (caramelised) — the key to biryani flavour. Set aside half.
2. Cook vegetables with whole spices, yogurt, and biryani masala until 70% done.
3. Par-boil rice with whole spices and salt until 70% cooked.
4. Layer: vegetable base, rice, fried onions, saffron milk, mint.
5. Seal pot with dough or foil; cook on dum (low heat) 20 minutes.
Serves: 4. Vegetarian. Contains dairy.

SECTION 2: ITALIAN CUISINE

2.1 Pasta Fundamentals
Pasta shapes are designed to work with specific sauces:
- Long thin (spaghetti, linguine): smooth oil-based or thin tomato sauces.
- Hollow tubes (penne, rigatoni): chunky meat or vegetable sauces that enter the tube.
- Twisted (fusilli, rotini): thicker creamy or pesto sauces that cling to ridges.
- Sheet (lasagne, pappardelle): rich meat ragùs, baked applications.
Al dente means "to the tooth" — pasta should have a slight resistance when bitten.
Always cook pasta in generously salted boiling water (the water should taste like the sea).
Reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining — the starchy water is essential for emulsifying sauces.

2.2 Classic Tomato Basil Pasta
Ingredients: 400g spaghetti, 2 cans whole San Marzano tomatoes, 4 garlic cloves, 6 tbsp extra virgin olive oil, 20 fresh basil leaves, 1 tsp sugar, salt, black pepper, chili flakes (optional).
Instructions:
1. Crush garlic and gently warm in 4 tbsp olive oil — do not brown (bitter).
2. Add tomatoes (hand-crushed); add sugar. Simmer 20 minutes.
3. Cook pasta to al dente; reserve pasta water.
4. Toss pasta in sauce with basil and remaining olive oil. Add pasta water to loosen.
Vegan and dairy-free. Contains gluten.

2.3 Carbonara (Authentic Roman)
Authentic carbonara contains NO cream. The creaminess comes from emulsification.
Ingredients: 400g rigatoni or spaghetti, 200g guanciale (or pancetta), 4 egg yolks + 1 whole egg, 100g Pecorino Romano (finely grated), black pepper, pasta water.
Instructions:
1. Cook guanciale in its own fat over medium heat until crispy but not burnt. Remove from heat.
2. Whisk eggs with grated Pecorino and generous black pepper.
3. Cook pasta; reserve 2 cups pasta water before draining.
4. Toss hot pasta with guanciale fat off heat.
5. Add egg mixture; toss vigorously, adding pasta water splash by splash until glossy and creamy.
CRITICAL: do not add eggs to a hot pan — they will scramble. Work quickly off heat.
Contains: eggs, dairy (Pecorino), gluten. Not suitable for those with pork allergies.

SECTION 3: EAST ASIAN CUISINE

3.1 Japanese Dashi and Umami
Dashi is the foundational stock of Japanese cuisine, providing umami depth.
Kombu dashi: soak 10g kombu in 1L cold water 30 minutes; heat to just below boiling; remove kombu. Simplest, vegan.
Awase dashi: add 20g katsuobushi (bonito flakes) to kombu dashi, steep 5 minutes off heat; strain. Richer umami.
Use dashi in: miso soup, ramen base, noodle broth, simmered vegetables.
Umami-boosting ingredients: soy sauce, miso, mushrooms (especially shiitake, porcini), tomatoes, aged cheese.

3.2 Simple Vegetable Fried Rice
Ingredients: 300g day-old cooked rice (key! fresh rice = clumpy), 2 eggs (beaten), 150g mixed vegetables (peas, corn, carrot, spring onion), 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tsp ginger (grated), 2 garlic cloves, 2 tbsp vegetable oil, white pepper.
Instructions:
1. Heat wok over high heat until smoking. Add oil.
2. Scramble eggs lightly; push to side.
3. Add garlic and ginger; stir-fry 30 seconds.
4. Add vegetables; stir-fry 2 minutes.
5. Add cold rice; break up clumps; stir-fry vigorously 3-4 minutes.
6. Add soy sauce; toss to coat. Add sesame oil off heat.
Note: Standard soy sauce contains gluten (use tamari for gluten-free). Contains eggs.

SECTION 4: INGREDIENT SUBSTITUTION GUIDE

4.1 Common Substitutions
Buttermilk (1 cup): 1 cup milk + 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar (let sit 5 min).
Eggs (per egg, in baking): 1 flax egg (1 tbsp ground flax + 3 tbsp water); 1/4 cup applesauce; 60g silken tofu blended; 3 tbsp aquafaba (chickpea brine).
Butter: equal amount coconut oil (unrefined for flavour); vegan butter block.
Heavy cream: full-fat coconut milk (in sauces); cashew cream (blend soaked cashews with water).
Soy sauce (gluten-free): tamari; coconut aminos.
All-purpose flour (gluten-free): GF blend 1:1; almond flour (different ratios needed); chickpea flour (savoury applications).
White sugar: coconut sugar (1:1, darker colour); maple syrup (3/4 cup per cup, reduce liquids by 3 tbsp).
Honey (vegan): maple syrup (1:1); agave syrup (3/4:1, sweeter).
Parmesan (vegan): nutritional yeast + cashew blend; store-bought vegan Parmesan.

4.2 Thickening Agents
Cornflour/cornstarch: mix 1 tbsp with 2 tbsp cold water (slurry) per cup of liquid. Clear, glossy result. Gluten-free.
Plain flour: 2 tbsp per cup of liquid (for roux). Not gluten-free.
Arrowroot: equal substitute for cornstarch. Clear result. Gluten-free.
Agar-agar (vegan gelatin): 1 tsp agar powder = 1 tbsp gelatin. Must be boiled to activate.
Xanthan gum: 1/4 tsp per cup of gluten-free flour blend for binding.
