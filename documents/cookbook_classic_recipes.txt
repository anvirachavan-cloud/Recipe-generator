CULINARY COOKBOOK — CLASSIC & MODERN RECIPES
=============================================

CHAPTER 1: FOUNDATIONAL COOKING TECHNIQUES

1.1 The Maillard Reaction and Browning
The Maillard reaction is a chemical process between amino acids and reducing sugars that gives browned food its distinctive flavour. This reaction occurs between 140–165°C (280–330°F). To achieve proper browning: ensure the food surface is dry before searing, use a heavy-bottomed pan (cast iron is ideal), preheat the pan before adding fat, and avoid overcrowding the pan. Patience is key — resist the urge to move food too early.

1.2 Knife Skills
Proper knife technique is fundamental to consistent cooking. The pinch grip (holding the blade between thumb and forefinger just above the handle) provides the most control and reduces fatigue. The claw grip (curling fingertips inward) protects fingers when chopping. Master these cuts: brunoise (1/8-inch cubes), small dice (1/4-inch), medium dice (1/2-inch), julienne (2-inch matchsticks), chiffonade (fine ribbons for leafy herbs).

1.3 Stock Making
A proper stock is the backbone of great cooking. For chicken stock: place 2 kg chicken bones in a pot, cover with cold water, bring to a boil, skim impurities, add 2 onions (quartered), 3 carrots, 3 celery stalks, a bouquet garni (thyme, bay leaves, parsley stems), and 10 peppercorns. Simmer uncovered for 3-4 hours. Strain and cool rapidly. Never boil stock vigorously — this creates a cloudy, greasy result.

CHAPTER 2: VEGETARIAN RECIPES

2.1 Classic Margherita Pizza
Ingredients: 250g pizza dough, 100ml San Marzano tomato sauce, 125g fresh mozzarella, fresh basil leaves, 2 tbsp olive oil, salt, black pepper.
Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to its highest setting (ideally 250°C/480°F) with a pizza stone inside for at least 30 minutes.
2. Stretch dough on a lightly floured surface to a 30cm circle.
3. Spread tomato sauce, leaving a 2cm border.
4. Tear mozzarella and distribute evenly.
5. Bake 8-10 minutes until crust is golden and cheese is bubbly.
6. Top with fresh basil and a drizzle of olive oil.
Serves: 2. Prep: 15 min. Cook: 10 min.

2.2 Lentil Dal
Ingredients: 200g red lentils (rinsed), 1 onion (diced), 3 garlic cloves, 1-inch ginger (grated), 2 tomatoes (chopped), 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp coriander powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp chili powder, 2 tbsp ghee or vegetable oil, salt to taste, fresh coriander to garnish.
Instructions:
1. Soak lentils 20 minutes; drain.
2. Heat ghee, add cumin seeds until they splutter.
3. Sauté onions until golden (8 min), add garlic and ginger (2 min).
4. Add spices and tomatoes; cook 5 minutes.
5. Add lentils and 600ml water; bring to boil.
6. Simmer 20-25 minutes until lentils are soft and creamy.
7. Adjust seasoning; garnish with coriander.
Serves: 4. Vegan if using oil. Naturally gluten-free.

2.3 Mushroom Risotto
Ingredients: 300g Arborio rice, 250g mixed mushrooms (cremini, shiitake, porcini), 1 shallot (minced), 2 garlic cloves, 100ml dry white wine, 1L warm vegetable stock, 50g Parmesan (grated), 30g butter, 2 tbsp olive oil, fresh thyme, salt and pepper.
Instructions:
1. Sauté mushrooms in 1 tbsp oil over high heat until golden. Set aside.
2. In same pan, soften shallot in remaining oil and butter (4 min).
3. Toast rice 2 minutes until translucent at edges.
4. Add wine; stir until absorbed.
5. Add stock one ladle at a time, stirring continuously, waiting for each ladle to absorb before adding the next (18-20 min total).
6. Stir in mushrooms, Parmesan, and remaining butter. Rest 2 minutes.
7. Season and serve immediately.
NOTE: Contains dairy (butter, Parmesan). Vegan substitution: use vegan butter and nutritional yeast.

CHAPTER 3: NON-VEGETARIAN RECIPES

3.1 Roast Chicken
A perfectly roasted chicken is the benchmark of a cook's skill.
Ingredients: 1 whole chicken (1.5kg), 2 tbsp softened butter, 1 lemon (halved), 4 garlic cloves, fresh rosemary and thyme, salt and pepper.
Instructions:
1. Allow chicken to reach room temperature (30 min out of fridge).
2. Preheat oven to 220°C/425°F.
3. Rub entire chicken (including under skin of breast) with seasoned butter.
4. Stuff cavity with lemon halves, garlic, and herbs.
5. Roast 15 minutes at 220°C, then reduce to 180°C/350°F.
6. Continue roasting 45-55 minutes until juices run clear.
7. FOOD SAFETY: Internal temperature must reach 74°C/165°F at thickest part of thigh using a meat thermometer.
8. Rest 15 minutes before carving.
Serves: 4. Prep: 15 min. Cook: 60-70 min.
ALLERGY NOTE: Contains dairy (butter). Gluten-free.

3.2 Pan-Seared Salmon with Lemon Butter
Ingredients: 2 salmon fillets (150g each), 2 tbsp olive oil, 30g butter, 1 lemon, 2 garlic cloves (sliced), capers, fresh dill.
Instructions:
1. Pat salmon completely dry; season generously with salt and pepper.
2. Heat oil in non-stick pan over medium-high heat until shimmering.
3. Place salmon skin-side down; press gently for 30 seconds.
4. Cook 4 minutes without moving until skin is crisp.
5. Flip; add butter, garlic, and a squeeze of lemon.
6. Baste with butter 2-3 minutes.
7. FOOD SAFETY: Internal temperature should reach 63°C/145°F.
Serves: 2. Contains fish, dairy.

3.3 Classic Beef Burger
Ingredients: 400g ground beef (80/20 lean/fat), salt, black pepper, garlic powder, 2 burger buns, lettuce, tomato, onion, cheese (optional).
Instructions:
1. Divide beef into two 200g portions; form gently into patties. Do not overwork.
2. Season generously on both sides just before cooking.
3. Cook on hot cast-iron grill or pan: 3-4 min per side for medium.
4. FOOD SAFETY: Ground beef must reach internal temperature of 71°C/160°F. Never serve undercooked ground beef.
Serves: 2.

CHAPTER 4: BAKING FUNDAMENTALS

4.1 The Science of Bread
Yeast-leavened bread requires four ingredients: flour, water, salt, and yeast. The gluten network (formed by mixing flour and water) traps CO2 produced by yeast fermentation, causing the dough to rise. Over-kneading = tough bread. Under-kneading = dense bread. Ideal proofing temperature: 27-32°C.

4.2 Basic Bread Recipe
Ingredients: 500g strong bread flour, 7g instant yeast, 10g salt, 330ml warm water (38°C), 2 tbsp olive oil.
Instructions:
1. Combine flour, yeast, salt. Add water and oil; mix to shaggy dough.
2. Knead 10 minutes until smooth and elastic (windowpane test).
3. Prove in oiled bowl, covered, 1 hour (or until doubled).
4. Shape, place in loaf tin, prove again 45 minutes.
5. Bake 200°C/390°F for 30-35 minutes. Hollow sound when tapped = done.
Contains: gluten (wheat). Not suitable for coeliacs.

4.3 Gluten-Free Banana Muffins
Ingredients: 3 ripe bananas (mashed), 200g gluten-free flour blend, 2 eggs, 80ml vegetable oil, 100g brown sugar, 1 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp baking soda, 1 tsp vanilla extract, pinch of salt.
Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 180°C/350°F; line muffin tin.
2. Whisk wet ingredients together.
3. Fold in dry ingredients until just combined (do not overmix).
4. Fill cups 3/4 full; bake 20-22 minutes.
Makes 12. Contains: eggs. Gluten-free, dairy-free. Can be made nut-free by checking flour brand.
